Creature Chess

How the pieces work
  • Your turn: move one piece (green dots), then capture with any piece you like (red) — it can be a different piece than the one that moved. You may skip either part.
  • Capturing does not move your piece — except the Void Walker, Dragonfly and King, which move onto the captured square.
  • Check is normal: your king can't be left where an enemy can capture it right now (only attacks count as check, not where enemies could move). No legal turn while in check = checkmate; no legal turn otherwise = stalemate. Since you move then capture, a king that gets captured loses the game too.
  • 🐙 Kraken (queen): moves 1 square any direction or 2 diagonally. Captures in all 8 directions up to 4 squares (first piece in the way).
  • 👤 Void Walker (pawn): moves 1 forward (or 2 forward if it hasn't moved yet); if a piece is in front it hops over to the square beyond. Moves to capture diagonally 1 square, or 2 squares (jumping a piece in the way). Never promotes.
  • ✂️ Scissors (bishop): moves diagonally any distance. Captures a forward cone: 3 squares in the first row, 5 in the second.
  • 👑 King: moves 1 square any direction; captures any adjacent piece and moves onto it.
  • 🐍 Snake (rook): moves orthogonally any distance. Captures all 8 adjacent squares.
  • 🪰 Dragonfly (knight): moves to any empty square exactly 2 away (jumping), and moves there to capture too.
